# Course Platform

Learners buy courses, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back within limits.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, customer

**Instructor**:
Someone who creates and teaches courses.
_Avoid_: Teacher, author

**Admin**:
Someone who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Moderator, staff

### Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A single payment that buys one course for good. There are no recurring payments.
_Avoid_: Subscription, order

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's access to a course they have purchased.
_Avoid_: Registration, membership

**Seat**:
One Learner's place in a course, bought as part of a team Purchase. A Seat is claimed once a Learner takes it.
_Avoid_: Coupon (for the place itself)

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's request to get the money back for a Purchase, decided by an Admin. It is Requested, Approved or Declined; a Purchase gets at most one, and it cannot be withdrawn.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, chargeback, reversal

**Refund**:
The money returned to the Learner once a Refund Request is approved: always the amount paid for the Purchase. A Refund ends the Enrollment; a declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase in which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cooling-off period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can still get a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, not a claimed Seat, no earlier Refund Request, and not a repeat Purchase of a course already refunded.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
